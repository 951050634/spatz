# Paper Workspaces

```text
papers/
├── paper-a/
│   └── paper_a.tex
├── paper-b/
│   └── paper_b.tex
└── paper-ab/
    └── paper_ab.tex
```

Paper A is the restricted merge-update prototype paper.

Paper B is reserved for the complete online softmax / attention extension. It
must not claim complete attention acceleration until the datapath and
end-to-end evidence exist.

Paper A+B combines the restricted integration baseline and full mixed-scalar
datapath into one system-prototype article.
