## Source policy

Generate numeric figures/tables from formal CSV, not transcribed prose. Use raw logs
for spot validation and exact equality/command traces. Architecture diagrams may use
RTL/report facts but must not invent unimplemented blocks. Existing superseded plots
under `experiments/plots/` are not source evidence and must be regenerated if reused.
