# SUPERSEDED — P9–P11 Derived Latency / Throughput / Area Efficiency

**SUPERSEDED / DO NOT USE FOR PAPER.** These historical artifacts derive
absolute latency and throughput from a common frequency assumption that was
withdrawn by the P7-R synchronous timing audit.  The files remain in place for
provenance and are listed in `experiments/parsed/SUPERSEDED_EVIDENCE.md`.

No active final record reports absolute latency, throughput, or area
efficiency.  Active workload evidence is measured cycles and cycle speedup in
`experiments/parsed/final_workload_comparison.csv`; active area and timing
boundaries are in `final_area.csv` and `final_timing.csv`.

The historical source files are:

- `experiments/parsed/p9_p11/p9_latency.csv`
- `experiments/parsed/p9_p11/p10_throughput.csv`
- `experiments/parsed/p9_p11/p11_area_efficiency.csv`

M4 may regenerate figures only after the final source-of-truth policy is
accepted.  This report is not an active source and its former numeric tables
must not be cited.
